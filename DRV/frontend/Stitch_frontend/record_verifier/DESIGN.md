## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the quiet, incorruptible authority of an institutional notary and university registrar. Rejecting speculative web3 aesthetic tropes—such as neon glow gradients, dark futuristic glass, and decorative floating matter—it draws deeply from the deliberate restraint of physical archival practices, official gazettes, and parliamentary parchment.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on three core tenets:
- **Assured:** Communication is calm, direct, and unhurried. The system speaks through measured human statements rather than hype or alarming alerts.
- **Quiet:** Visual noise is stripped away. Generous breathing space, disciplined warm paper tones, and 1px hairlines guide attention toward verification truths.
- **Precise:** Cryptographic hashes, validation pipelines, and archival timestamps are treated with typographical rigor, ensuring machine proofs and human legibility coexist seamlessly.

The visual execution pairs warm tactile paper backgrounds with deep parliamentary ink-navy text, grounded by an austere muted teal accent. Elevation is tactile and shallow, evoking physical paper slips, certified stamps, and notary ledgers laid across an oak desk.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around classical archival media: unbleached cotton rag paper, iron gall ink, and a single muted teal seal pigment.

### Chromatic Discipline
- **Primary Ink (`#1B2A41`):** Deep navy ink reserved for headlines, institutional wordmarks, and primary seals.
- **Secondary Accent (`#285F5B`):** A muted archival teal utilized exclusively for intentional interactive elements (primary actions, active navigation states, and deliberate focus rings). Strictly one accent color is displayed per functional view.
- **Neutral Grounds (`#F6F4EF`, `#FFFFFF`, `#EFECE4`):** Warm off-white paper canvas with stark white raised card containers and subdued oyster gray inset layers for code blocks, tables, and hash containers.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#DDD8CC`):** Crisp 1px structural outlines separating distinct functional planes without heavy contrast.

### Verification States
Status colors are never employed in isolation; they are permanently paired with an icon, semantic badge background, and unambiguous descriptive prose.
- **Verified:** Deep forest green (`#2F6B4A`) on pale sage (`#E5F0E9`).
- **Mismatch:** Terracotta brick (`#8E3A31`) on pale blush (`#F5E1DD`).
- **Revoked:** Antique ochre (`#7A5410`) on muted parchment (`#F6ECD6`).
- **Not Found:** Slate maritime (`#34506B`) on muted mist (`#E4EBF2`).
- **Needs Clarification:** Neutral charcoal (`#5B6670`) on warm gray (`#ECEAE3`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a rigorous distinction between institutional human authority, clear user interface guidance, and unvarnished machine proofs.

- **Headings (Libre Caslon Text):** Rooted in classic letterpress proportions, the serif display conveys official weight, pedigree, and calm permanence. It anchors views, sectional headers, and verification outcomes.
- **Interface & Body (IBM Plex Sans):** Systematic, legible, and devoid of whimsical styling. Body text maintains a strict typographic measure of at most 68 characters per line to safeguard reading comfort. All numeric timestamps, currency, and IDs activate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- **Hashes, JSON & Ledger Proofs (JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono):** Applied universally to cryptographic digests, contract addresses, structured queries, and file checksums. Hashes are divided visually into readable 8-character groupings.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an orderly 4px/8px incremental rhythm within a bounded reading frame.

### Grid Architecture
- **Max Container Width:** 1120px centered canvas, maintaining a cohesive ledger document presence across wide screens.
- **Desktop (>= 1120px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and exterior margins.
- **Tablet (641px - 1119px):** 8-column layout with fluid redistribution; side panels (such as execution traces and parsed JSON) stack underneath primary inquiry forms at the 900px threshold.
- **Mobile (<= 640px):** Single-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) exterior margins, ensuring tap targets remain >= 44px.

### Sectional Cadence
Form fields, dropzones, and output cards are unified through standard `1.5rem` (`space-xl`) internal padding. Vertical spacing between independent modules defaults to `3rem` (`space-3xl`) to instill a quiet, deliberate tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical, tactile cues without heavy blurs, artificial glow effects, or decorative floating depths.

- **Flat Canvas (`--bg`):** Ground level warm paper tone (`#F6F4EF`).
- **Resting Containers (`--surface`):** Crisp pure white card sheets framed with a 1px solid hairline (`#DDD8CC`) and an ultra-subtle ink tint shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.06);`
- **Raised Interactive Cards:** Upon hover or selective focus, cards rise slightly (2px translation) with a steady ambient diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.08);`
- **Recessed / Inset Tiers (`--surface-subtle`):** Inset dropzones, JSON display trays, and hash verification boxes employ flat fills (`#EFECE4`) nested inside a 1px border with no drop shadow, communicating physical inlay.

## Shapes

The design system employs conservative, architectural corner treatments (Level 1: Soft). This geometry reinforces physical document slips, registry certificates, and index cards without yielding to generic round consumer app styles.

- **Interactive Inputs & Action Buttons:** 6px radius (`0.375rem`), presenting crisp, clean tactile bounds.
- **Cards, Modals & Inspection Panels:** 10px radius (`0.625rem`), providing clear containment while retaining crisp rectangular balance.
- **Badges & Inline Chips:** 6px radius (`0.375rem`), resisting pill-shaped bubbles to avoid casual aesthetics.
- **Full Circles (`999px`):** Strictly reserved for the circular verification seal icon, step sequence indicator nodes, and status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid muted teal background (`#285F5B`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), 44px min height, 6px radius, font weight 500. On hover, subtly deepens to `#1F4D49`. On press, undergoes `translateY(1px)`. Disabled state holds 50% opacity without cursor interaction.
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent fill, 1px hairline border (`#DDD8CC`), ink navy text (`#1B2A41`). On hover, surface tints to `#EFECE4`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** 44px height, 1px border (`#DDD8CC`), background `#FFFFFF`, font size 16px to prevent mobile zoom. Label situated precisely above in 13px medium (`#1F2933`), helper copy situated beneath in 13px regular (`#5B6670`). Active focus displays a crisp 2px offset ring in muted teal (`#285F5B`).

### Dropzone
- **Verification Intake:** Inset `#EFECE4` background bordered by a 1.5px dashed border (`#DDD8CC`). Hover and drag-over transitions smoothly to a solid 1.5px border with `#E2EEEC` tint. Displays centered document icon, clear text directives ("Drop certificate PDF or browse"), accepted extensions, and max file thresholds. Upon upload, transitions immediately to an active chip showing file name, byte size, and removal trigger.

### Verification Result Card
- **Layout & Structure:** Prominently displays the 56px official seal mark. The outcome title is rendered in 28px Caslon serif accompanied by an unambiguous summary sentence. 
- **Hash Inspection Blocks:** Displays computed hash alongside registered blockchain hash in twin monospace blocks. Grouped into 8-character chunks for human eye comparison. Matching characters inherit soft green highlighting; differing characters on mismatch highlight sharply in terracotta brick. Includes quick-copy button.
- **Statutory Disclaimer:** Permanently pins the mandatory disclaimer at the base of every verification view: *"A match shows the file is identical to what was registered. It doesn't prove who issued it or that its contents are true."*

### Validation Pipeline Stepper
- **Layout:** Horizontal sequence linking 5 discrete nodes: *Schema → Intent Allowlist → Record ID → Role Authority → Contract Call*.
- **States:** Idle (hairline circle), Active (pulsing outline), Passed (teal fill with checkmark), Failed (terracotta shake with explicit error callout).

### Tables & Archival Logs
- **Registry Tables:** Hairline divider lines, subtle zebra striping with `#EFECE4`, sticky header, 13px labels free from aggressive uppercase letter-spacing, numbers set in tabular monospaced figures.
