Rules Baseline (Regex)

Deterministic
  • Latency: ~1.0 ms (instantaneous regex parsing)
  • Grammar Flexibility: Rigid keyword heuristics; drops on informal queries
  • Safety: Strict fallback to unknown intent
  • Resource Footprint: Zero GPU/VRAM requirement

Small Language Model (Qwen2.5-3B)

Semantic
  • Latency: ~3.2 s on RTX 4050 Laptop GPU
  • Grammar Flexibility: High resilience to synonyms, colloquialisms, typos
  • Zero-Trust Safety: Anti-hallucination + Stage 1-5 validation
  • Resource Footprint: ~1.9 GB VRAM, 4-bit quantized

Integrity Notice: A match shows the file is identical to what was registered. It doesn't prove who issued it or that its contents are true.
